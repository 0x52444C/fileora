Fileora Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

Fileora is an offline, bring-your-own-data game player. This policy covers what the app does and does not do with your information.

Data collection

Fileora does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to the developer. There is no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party SDKs of any kind in the app.

Importing your own data

On first launch, Fileora asks you to supply your own previously-converted game data, either by:

  • picking a folder already on your device, or
  • downloading it from an address on your own local network that you type in yourself.

Either way, this transfer happens directly between your own devices (or within your own device's storage) and never passes through, or is visible to, the developer in any way.

Multiplayer

Fileora's optional multiplayer mode connects directly to an address (or a nearby device over Bluetooth / local network) that you provide, for peer-to-peer play between the devices involved. The developer does not operate, and has no access to, any central server, and does not receive or store any information from these sessions.

Permissions

Local Network and Bluetooth permissions, if requested, are used only to discover and connect to nearby devices for multiplayer and for the local-network data import option above.

Children's privacy

Fileora does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children, because it does not collect information at all.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this same page.
